From hospital images to the genome

I take AI out of the lab and into the places where it has to work — radiotherapy, radiology and, now, drug discovery.

I studied computer science at the Universidad de Oviedo and wrote my master’s thesis at the University of Cambridge. After engineering roles at Telefónica I+D, Redgate, Citrix and Featurespace, I joined Microsoft in 2012 and built Skype’s real-time telemetry pipeline, later scaled across Xbox, Bing and Office.

For 10 years at Microsoft Research I worked on AI for medicine. I led Project InnerEye — radiotherapy AI that cut contouring time by 36–67% in NHS use, open-sourced and adopted by 30+ organisations — and AI engineering through FDA 510(k) clearance. As Senior Director in Health Futures I led a 13-person team across the UK, US and India building multimodal foundation models, collaborating with Mayo Clinic to deploy draft reporting for chest X-rays, and with Novartis and Cyted.

In 2026, as an advisor, I worked on autonomous chest X-ray reporting and built the knowledge-base layer of the open-source Firefly Agentic framework. Today I am Director of AI at Quotient Therapeutics, a Flagship Pioneering company using somatic genomics to discover medicines.
